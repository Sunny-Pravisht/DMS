Ask anything about your documents

Permission-scoped

Answers are generated from your repository. Verify anything you rely on for a legal or financial decision.

What the assistant can do

Find
Locate documents by meaning, not keywords
Summarise
Condense long documents and whole sets
Extract
Pull out numbers, dates and parties
Act
Route a document into a workflow from chat

Scope
